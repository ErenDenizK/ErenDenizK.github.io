This is a sample essay. It is here so the long reading layout can be tested with text that behaves like a
real essay: paragraphs of different lengths, a heading or two, a figure, a list and a closing line. The
words pretend to describe how I work with agents, but nothing here is a claim yet.

The short version: I decide what to build and why, I write the brief, and I review everything that comes
back. The agents type. The work is in the deciding and the reviewing.

## What directing means

<Figure name="entry-flow" />

The figure above stands for the loop: a brief goes out, work comes back, I look at it, and either it ships
or the brief gets sharper. Most of the time is spent in the looking.

- A brief says what, why and what done looks like.
- Review means running it, not reading it.
- A rejected round is information, not waste.

## What it is not

It is not pressing a button and shipping what comes out. A paragraph like this one runs long enough to
wrap over several lines on a desktop screen, so the line length and the leading can be judged at the
reading size.

The end of the sample essay.
