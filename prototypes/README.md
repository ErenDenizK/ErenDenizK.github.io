Status: history (closed 2026-10-08/09). The first design directions, each a single page: A to D
(`a-die`, `b-glass-lab`, `c-canvas`, `d-quiet`, with the shared copy in `CONTENT.md`), then E
(`e-dark`, Direction D on a dark ground, with its objects and `rig.js`) and F (`f`, E rebuilt to the
owner's verdict, and `f-log`). The site grew from them (ADR-0003, ADR-0007); none of these pages is
live. They are kept, not moved, because ADRs, research files, code comments and `tools/objects`
(which reads `e-dark/objects/rig.js`) cite them by path, about thirty references in all. Do not edit
them; a new design goes to a lab page (`src/pages/lab/`). `shoot.mjs` took their screenshots into
the git-ignored `*/shots/` folders.
