# Brief

brief (2026-10-08) and every dated amendment since. Each line carries the date of the statement
it rests on; when two statements disagree, the newest wins. The original text and every
amendment, verbatim, are in the decision log, [`docs/history/brief-log.md`](history/brief-log.md);
older citations of the form "brief §N" point at its sections. To change intent: append a dated
amendment to the log, then update the line here. Decisions about the other products (Recto,
English Prep, Eat Map) live in their own repos and `docs/family-kit/SESSIONS.md`, not here.

### Way of working

- Work is split into separate chats; this chat focuses only on the portfolio. (2026-10-10)
- Start with a clean head: discard earlier rounds, do a fresh situation analysis, fix wrong
  CLAUDE.md, ADRs and docs; keep an evaluation board; report regularly. (2026-10-10)
- Review everything with its purpose, its reason and a designer's discipline. (2026-10-10)
- The agent leads (plans, research, agents, tooling, stack); the owner gives direction, taste
  and final say. Step by step, no deadline. (2026-10-08) "Read the tasks and answers fully,
  assign agents across branches, set up the system: you are the boss of this work." (2026-10-11)
- Before any new task after the rejected Home round: a detailed analysis of why the output was
  so bad. (2026-10-11, late)
- Desktop first: the owner tests desktop until it is right, then phones. Both matter.
  (2026-10-09)
- The 2026-10-11 rounds were tested on the phone only. (2026-10-11, afternoon) Both platforms
  matter: a phone when the owner shares the site in person, a computer when an employer or a
  teacher opens it. (2026-10-15)
- A production system: plans, logs and reports so context does not rot; stale docs refreshed,
  unneeded files archived, CLAUDE.md reviewed regularly; a new CLAUDE.md rule only after the
  owner's comment. The owner's dictation is turned into clean bullet points and improved.
