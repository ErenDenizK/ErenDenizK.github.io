## The idea

Typography and light are the hero; the glass `edk` stays in the line of the name, read as a word
("Eren Deniz **edk** / Kuyucaklıoğlu."). The page is an index of the maker that is visibly alive.

## Answers to the research's critique

Scroll-linked and reversible; the bar does not tuck on phones while it happens. |

## Live details (all real)

Istanbul's sun is computed in the page (short NOAA formula, no service): the phase ("Night in Istanbul"),
the next sunrise or sunset, a small sun on an arc, and the sky over the name tinted by the sun's altitude.
The latest record entry says how long ago ("3 days ago"); the record shows the year as a strip of
project-coloured ticks and the four newest entries.

## Still twins

- No JavaScript: the composition is CSS and ends in the final state; the sky is a neutral dusk; the acts
  are plain sections one under another; the dot is not a button (it is created by the script).
- Reduced motion or `?still`: no composition, no sweep, no docking, the light stands where the sun is;
  acts switch without movement; the dot still changes colour.
- Once per visit: a second Home in the same tab arrives composed (`sessionStorage`).

## Technique notes

- Blending: `main` is isolated over its own ground so the lens (multiply) and the objects (plus-lighter)
  blend against the real ground; the sticky stage paints its own ground (as ADR-0014 item 6). Inside an act
  nothing that wraps an object takes opacity, a transform or perspective (each makes a group and the
  object loses the ground, found as white and black boxes while building); the parts fade on their own.
- Budget: measured as the index, B was 192.6 KB at first paint (limit 200; the old Home 181.9): HTML +6 KB gz (letters, stage), JS +4 KB
  (`scripts/home-b.ts`). Captures are lazy. The old Home's `showcase.css` and hero rules in `global.css` are gone (unused by A).
- Tests: `tests/e2e/home-b.spec.ts`. `sheet.ts` also opens the project sheet on a page that marks a
  `[data-sheet-host]` (this lab page); `site.ts` keeps the phone bar in place while the name docks.
