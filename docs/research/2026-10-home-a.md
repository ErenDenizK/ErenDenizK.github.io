# Home, direction A: the cinematic object stage (2026-10-11)

**Status:** a built prototype on `wip/home-a`, for the lead to compare with direction B (`wip/home-b`)
and with the research (`docs/research/2026-10-home-award.md`, on `wip/homeres`). Technique decided by the
agent; the look and the vision line are the owner's to judge. The vision line is the lead's **draft**
(`site.json home.visionDraft`, shown with a dashed "draft" tag) until the owner writes one.
**Rests on:** brief, Home (2026-10-11 evening: "a stunning opening page", a design-award feel; 2026-10-10:
Home presents a product, an identity, a brand and the vision); ADR-0006 (pre-rendered objects, the 1° grid,
sharpness), ADR-0007 (routing, the stage morph), ADR-0013 (the rail), ADR-0014 (real screens in frames).

## Concept

The edk glass is the hero and the brand, and it never leaves. The page is a stage: one composed first
screen, then a story in chapters that pass the pinned glass while the glass turns with the reading
position, changes into each product's own glass object, and is lit in each chapter's colour. Light is
colour, never paint: every light is an additive radial pool (plus-lighter) over the black ground.

Three planes (styles/home.css): the big words (the name, the two tracks, each product's wordmark) lie
**under** the glass, so the glass adds its light onto the letters that pass behind it; the stage (light and
glass, one additive group) above them; the reading text above that, always beside the glass, never under it.

1. **First screen, one frame.** The glass in the middle (705 px at 1x on a 1440 x 900 window, 600 at 2x,
   500 on smaller windows; the poster widths, so the still is drawn 1:1), "Eren Deniz" closing on it from
   above and "Kuyucaklıoğlu" from below, crossing under its square; the vision line bottom left; the
   course line; Istanbul and its time in the display face (the bar's own clock rests while this one shows);
   "See the work", GitHub, LinkedIn; a quiet scroll cue; "Click the glass" until the first press.
2. **Who** (cool white-blue, gold low left): one claim and two lines; three facts beside the glass. The
   glass looks toward the text.
3. **How I work** (gold, with blue): "By hand" and "With agents" set huge under the glass, one at each
   side, with the two lanes (About's data, reused). The glass turns from the left lane (-16°) to the right
   (+16°) as the chapter passes: the real 1° pose grid, driven by scroll instead of the pointer.
4. **What I build**, then **one chapter per product** in its own light (Recto lime; English Prep sakura with
   iris and lagoon; Eat Map rose with wine and pink): the glass changes into the product's object (the
   site's one-object-changes-into-the-next rule), its wordmark passes under it, and beside it the status,
   the pitch, the real numbers, "Take a look" (the focus view) and its live link, and its real screen in a
   frame: Recto in a browser frame with its address, English Prep on a phone; Eat Map shows none until it
   has captures (nothing is invented). Order: `featured` first, then `order`.
5. **The record** (blue): the latest three entries with their project marks; the whole record.
6. **Next** (edk again): the glass at rest between two doors, Work and About, and the "Now" line.

**Phones** (390 x 844, the owner's iPhone): the same story in one column. The first screen is the glass
large at the top (min(96vw, 400 px), its spin loop playing), the name under it, the vision line, and at the
bottom, in thumb reach, Istanbul's time and a full-width "See the work". On the first scroll the glass
shrinks into an emblem under the bar (to 0.42, scroll-linked) and stays there through the story, changing
into each product and taking each chapter's light; a veil of ground takes the reading text that passes
under it. Every way in is a 52 px button in the reading flow.

## Choreography

| moment | what moves | timing |
|---|---|---|
| arrival | the light blooms from 0.55x scale and nothing (the poster is painted at once, untouched) | 2.4 s, ease-out, 45 % to full opacity |
| arrival | the name rises line by line from 0.22 em with a 10 px blur; vision line, course line, last row follow | 1.3 s / 1.1 s, staggered 120 ms from 120 ms |
| arrival | one slow sweep of light across the glass (media.ts `arrive`) | at 650 ms, 1.8 s; not when arriving through the droplet |
| arrival | the scroll cue's line draws down and fades | 2.4 s loop from 1.8 s |
| scroll | the glass's pose: keys at each chapter's centre (several spread over ±32 % of a chapter), eased between, on media.ts's spring (ζ 0.9, about 300 ms), settling on an exact 1° frame when the page stops | continuous |
| scroll | the chapter at the middle of the window sets the light (three registered colours) and the page accent (dot, tab, rail) | 1.2 / 1.5 / 1.8 s glides |
| scroll | the glass changes object once a chapter has held the middle for 280 ms (a fling changes nothing) | media.ts's 360 ms out, 480 ms in from 240 ms |
| scroll | reading text arrives as it enters | 0.9 s opacity, 1.1 s from 28 px |
| phone scroll | the glass becomes the emblem over the first 62 % of the first screen | scroll-linked, smoothstep |
| press | the hop (media.ts's interaction clip, from the rest pose) and a ring of the chapter's light | 1.1 s |

Still twins: reduced motion, Save-Data and `?still` keep posters, no entrance, no pose, no emblem (phones
show the glass in the first screen only, unpinned), object changes as 150 ms crossfades. Without
JavaScript every chapter is there, the edk glass pinned on wide screens, and each product shows its own
poster beside its text. Forced colours: the stage is unpinned and unblended.

## Technique (decided by the agent)

- `media.ts` gains one additive hook, `MediaStage.pose(slot, {mx, my} | null)`, read in its existing
  pointer aim: the page's pose is used when no pointer is near, and a pointer near the glass takes over (and
  hands back when it leaves). Nothing else in media.ts changed (the spin-loop work on `wip/spins` touches
  other functions; no overlap).
- The stage is a solo slot. On a desktop with a fine pointer only edk goes past its poster: its lean grid is
  what the scroll turns. A product shown on the stage stays a still there, so Home never fetches a
  product's grid (1.3-1.9 MB each). On touch screens every object keeps its loop.
- `#media-index` (built from the manifests) lets the stage change into the products.
- The scroll is never taken over: one rAF handler reads positions; poses and lights are written, scroll is
  not. No scroll-driven CSS timelines (Safari support), no loader, no frames on the critical path.

## Measured (headless Chromium; `npm run budget`, Playwright)

- **First paint** (tests/budget.mjs): Home 188.3 KB (HTML 34.9, fonts 117.5, JS 19.8 gz, images 16.2)
  against the 200 KB limit. The poster is the LCP element and is never faded.
- **Moving media on a desktop**: edk's grid only (the half and full tiers, about 1.4 MB, as before); the old
  Home also fetched Recto's showcase grid (the research measured 3.1 MB of idle grids on Home), which is
  gone. Product objects cost their posters (11-17 KB each) and only when their chapter holds.
- **Phones**: posters, the edk spin loop, the captures as their chapters near (lazy).
- **Page length**: 1440 x 900 about 9,100 px (10 screens); 390 x 844 about 6,700 px.
- **Scroll cost** (headless Chromium, software compositing, a machine under load): about 90 ms a frame on
  Home against 65 on Work and 45 on About, the price of a full-window additive stage over the page. Not a
  real GPU number; to measure on the owner's laptop and iPhone before this ships.
- The glass covers 705 x 705 px of 1440 x 900 at 1x (the letters about 500 x 200), against 470 px before.

## Not done, and why

- **Larger and sharper at 2x.** The posters stop at 1200 px, so at DPR 2 the slot is capped at 600 CSS px;
  a hero at 75 % of the width (the research's concept) needs a new hero render (`tools/objects`, poster
  and grid at about 2000 px). Not done here: another agent is changing the object media.
- **The letters do not travel or shrink on desktop.** The pinned glass stays whole and sharp; a CSS scale of
  the lean canvas would resample it (ADR-0006 evening amendment). Phones, which play video, do shrink it.
- **A person chapter** with a photograph (the research's gold section) is left for the owner's call.
- Safari, Firefox and a real iPhone are unverified; all numbers are headless Chromium.

## Tests

`tests/e2e/home.spec.ts`: the first screen's composition; chapters and rail sections in order; products by
wordmark, way in and real screen; the pinned glass changing into each product and back with the accent; a
fling changes nothing; the press and the hint; the tablet column; the phone emblem and thumb reach;
reduced motion; no JavaScript. `stage.spec.ts` now checks that the reading position turns the glass on its
grid (the scroll re-aim of the unpinned object no longer applies). `live.spec.ts` drops the showcase test:
Home has no showcase slot.
