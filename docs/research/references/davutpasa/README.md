# YTU Davutpaşa campus: reference photos

Supplied by the owner on 2026-10-17 as references for the Davutpaşa engraving (row S46 in
`docs/feedback/2026-10-16-lab-one-review.md`). They are references only: never publish them on the
site, because their authors are not known.

The subject is the **Orta Bahçe** (the central courtyard): the long rectangular historic barracks
building with a red tiled roof frames a garden of trees and paths in a symmetric, partly radial
plan, with a small pavilion near the centre. It is iconic for the campus and a place where
students spend much of their time.

- `aerial-night-courtyard.webp`: the courtyard at night from above, the city behind.
- `aerial-courtyard-autumn.webp`: the courtyard and its paths in autumn, from above.
- `aerial-dusk-wide.webp`: the whole rectangle at dusk, with the sports field and the city.
- `top-down-courtyard.webp`: the path plan seen straight down.
- `facade-entrance.webp`: the building's facade, arched windows and the entrance, from the garden.
